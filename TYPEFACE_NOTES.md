# Type-face direction (v3)

The renderer was adjusted after reviewing historic typewriter references.

Design principles:
- Pica / Elite proportions rather than a distressed handwriting effect.
- Straight mechanical baseline inside a line.
- Fixed wear belongs to a physical key: the same `e` repeats the same tiny defect.
- Ribbon pressure varies per strike.
- Human/mechanical irregularity is expressed through line starts, word spacing,
  occasional rebound/double strike, and ink transfer.
- No per-character vertical wobble.

The app's "Davoren Pica" and "Davoren Elite" are procedural machine faces, not
copies of a commercial distressed font and not distributed as TTF/OTF files.
